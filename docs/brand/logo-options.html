Watchman marks

Six marks drawn from the subject, three wordmark faces, and three colour directions shown on a working portal fragment and a digest header. Nothing here is purple or olive. The portal chrome itself stays ink and white in every direction; the hue appears only on the mark and on the main data element.

Marks

Watchman

1. Beam

A lighthouse from above. The tower is the watchman; the beam is what it gives. Reads at 16 px, works in one colour.

Watchman

2. Veil

The product's own output: a frame with a blur patch inside. The only mark here that says what the thing does. The soft edge is lost at 16 px.

Watchman

3. Wick

A W whose middle peak carries a flame. The lantern kept lit through the night. A monogram, so it ties to the name.

Watchman

4. Window

The one lit window in a dark house. Household, night, someone awake. Reads as a door or a phone too, which may help.

Watchman

5. Dawn

The watch ends at first light, when the digest goes out. Calm rather than vigilant. The gentlest of the six.

Watchman

6. Two

The protected person and the partner. The overlap is what both see. Honest about what the app is: a relationship, not a camera.

Wordmark

WatchmanGeist 500, the portal's own face. One typeface for everything, which the ledger style prefers.
WatchmanBricolage Grotesque 600. More character in the W and the a; the UI stays in Geist.
WatchmanInstrument Serif. Quiet and bookish, like a night log. A second face used only for the name.

Colour directions

Each direction keeps the same chrome: an off-white ground tinted from the direction's world, white cards, ink text, one hue. The hue goes on the mark, on today's bar in the protected-hours chart, and on the one number in the digest. The pill is the same amber notice in all three because it means "needs review", not brand.

A. Lamplight

Warm paper ground, lantern amber. The plan document already leans this way. Amber is also the colour of a notice, so the review pill and the hue sit close; the fragment shows how that feels.

My pick, if you want one

  1. Mark: Beam (1) or Wick (3). Beam is the strongest at 16 px and says "watch" without an eye. Wick ties to the name and to the lamplight direction.
  2. Colour: Harbour (B). It is unlike the usual dashboard palettes, and the amber of a notice stays free to mean "look at this".
  3. Wordmark: Geist, so the product has one face everywhere, including the phone and the emails.
